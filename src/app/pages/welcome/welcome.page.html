Privacy Policy Proculus

Last Updated: June 2026

About Proculus

Proculus is an e-Procurement platform designed to support digital procurement processes. Through this application, vendors can register accounts, manage company profiles, upload supporting documents, access tender information, participate in bidding processes, and monitor procurement results efficiently and transparently.

By using Proculus, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Encrypted Password

Vendor Information

  • Company Name
  • Company Address
  • Business Documents
  • Tax Information, if required
  • Vendor Verification Data

Tender and Bidding Information

  • Tender Participation Records
  • Bid Submissions
  • Tender Activity History
  • Procurement Related Information

Technical Information

  • Device Information
  • Operating System
  • IP Address
  • Login Activity
  • Application Usage Logs

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used to:

  • Create and manage vendor accounts
  • Verify vendor identity and eligibility
  • Process tender participation
  • Process bid submissions
  • Manage procurement activities
  • Improve platform functionality
  • Ensure security and fraud prevention
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell users' personal information to third parties.

3. Document Uploads

Documents uploaded by vendors may be used for:

  • Vendor verification
  • Tender requirements
  • Administrative evaluation
  • Procurement processes

Uploaded documents are only accessible to authorized personnel involved in procurement activities.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect user information, including password encryption, secure authentication, access control mechanisms, protected databases and servers, security monitoring, and logging.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

User information is retained only as long as necessary to provide application services, maintain procurement records, fulfill legal obligations, and resolve disputes or security issues.

6. Account Deletion

Users may request account deletion through the support channels provided by Proculus.

When an account deletion request is approved:

  • Personal account information may be removed.
  • Certain procurement records may be retained where required by law, auditing requirements, dispute resolution, or security purposes.

Account deletion requests are generally processed within 30 days.

7. User Rights

Users have the right to:

  • Access their information
  • Update profile information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request account deletion
  • Contact support regarding privacy concerns

8. Local Storage and Authentication

Proculus may use secure local device storage to:

  • Maintain login sessions
  • Store authentication tokens
  • Improve user experience

These mechanisms are required for application functionality.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in services, legal requirements, or security practices. Any updates will be published within the application.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Proculus support team through the support channels available within the application.

By creating an account or using Proculus, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy.
